About Me

I am an undergraduate student at Chongqing University, majoring in Data Science and Big Data Technology. My work connects AI for education, learning analytics, and trustworthy intelligent systems, with a focus on using data-driven and large-model methods to model learning behavior and support reliable educational decisions.

My recent research spans multi-agent reflection for fake news detection, multimodal misinformation detection, and LLM-based virtual student data generation. These projects trained me to build systems that combine model performance with interpretability, traceable evidence, and practical usefulness.

Looking ahead, I hope to continue exploring knowledge tracing, educational data generation, and large language models for education, with the goal of developing reliable AI systems that better understand learners and make personalized learning more trustworthy.

Research

Multi-Agent Self-Reflective Learning Framework for Fake News Detection

Highlight: A multi-agent system that accumulates, reuses, and refines detection experience through reflection.

Built a CrewAI-based collaborative network with a fast-judgment agent using fine-tuned BERT, a dynamic fact-checking agent, and a comprehensive reasoning agent. The framework combines active and conflict-triggered reflection with a dynamic experience base; on PolitiFact and GossipCop, it improved F1 by 5.1% and 14.0% over DEFEND, and also outperformed fine-tuned LLM baselines.

Multi-agent systems Reflection Experience memory Fake news detection
Multimodal Fake News Detection with MoE and Knowledge-enhanced Reasoning

Highlight: A multimodal expert-collaboration framework that integrates complementary text, image, audio, and video evidence.

Participated in building an MoE network with task routing, decision fusion, and 15 specialized models across text, image, audio, and video modalities. The method uses dynamic probabilistic weighting and RAG-based knowledge retrieval, achieving 88.11% accuracy and 84.60% F1-score on Weibo-21; the platform based on this algorithm won a national second prize.

LLM-based Virtual Student Response Generation and Performance Prediction

Highlight: Synthetic student response records that serve as effective supplements for knowledge tracing tasks.

Implemented a lightweight pipeline inspired by Agent4Edu, using ASSISTments 2009 concepts and difficulty signals to generate virtual student response records. After mixing synthetic and real records at a 1:4 ratio, DKT experiments under pyKT achieved 0.8726 AUC and 0.8176 ACC, improving the AUC by 6.2% over the real-data baseline.
