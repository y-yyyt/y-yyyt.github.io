Research

Multi-Agent Fake News Detection System with Reflection and Iterative Learning

Project Leader · Advisor: Prof. Min Gao
Multi-agent systems · Fake news detection · Reflection mechanism · Experience memory
  • Proposed a multi-agent fake news detection framework to improve cross-verification and self-correction in large-model-based detection systems.
  • Built a CrewAI-based agent workflow consisting of a fast judgment agent, a dynamic fact-checking agent, and a comprehensive reasoning agent.
  • Designed reflection mechanisms triggered by confidence scores and reasoning conflicts, and constructed a structured experience memory base for long-term improvement.
  • The system improved F1-scores on PolitiFact and GossipCop datasets compared with several baseline methods.

Multimodal Fake News Detection Based on MoE and Knowledge-enhanced Reasoning

Core Member · Advisor: Prof. Min Gao
Multimodal learning · Mixture of Experts · RAG · Knowledge-enhanced reasoning
  • Participated in the design of a multimodal fake news detection method combining MoE-based collaboration and knowledge-enhanced reasoning.
  • Built an expert collaboration network with task routing, result decision-making, and multiple domain experts.
  • Integrated fine-tuned multimodal expert models covering text, image, audio, and video information.
  • Achieved 88.11% accuracy and 84.60% F1-score on the Weibo-21 dataset.

LLM-based Virtual Student Response Generation and Performance Prediction

Independent Project
Educational AI · Student modeling · Synthetic data · Knowledge tracing
  • Explored the use of large language models to generate virtual student response records for educational modeling scenarios where real learning data may be limited or sensitive.
  • Implemented a lightweight pipeline inspired by Agent4Edu, including student profile construction and response behavior simulation.
  • Aligned generated records with the ASSISTments data format and trained deep knowledge tracing models under the pyKT framework.
