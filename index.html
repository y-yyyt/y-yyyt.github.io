Research Experience

Multi-Agent Fake News Detection System with Reflection and Iterative Learning

Project Leader
Multi-Agent System CrewAI Fake News Detection Reflection Mechanism Experience Memory
  • Proposed a multi-agent fake news detection framework to address the limitations of insufficient cross-verification and weak self-correction in existing large-model-based methods.
  • Built a collaborative agent network based on CrewAI, including a fast judgment agent, a dynamic fact-checking agent, and a comprehensive reasoning agent.
  • Designed active and passive reflection mechanisms triggered by confidence and reasoning conflicts, and constructed a dynamic experience memory base for long-term learning.
  • Achieved improved F1-scores on PolitiFact and GossipCop datasets compared with traditional and fine-tuned large-model baselines.

Multimodal Fake News Detection Based on MoE and Knowledge-enhanced Reasoning

Core Member
Mixture of Experts Multimodal Learning RAG Knowledge Base Misinformation Detection
  • Proposed a multimodal fake news detection method based on MoE and knowledge-enhanced reasoning to improve interpretability, traceability, and generalization.
  • Built a collaborative MoE network with task routing, result decision-making, and six domain experts.
  • Integrated 15 fine-tuned multimodal expert models covering text, image, audio, and video modalities.
  • Constructed a public opinion and domain knowledge base by collecting and restructuring authoritative rumor-refutation resources and open-source rumor datasets.
  • The system achieved 88.11% accuracy and 84.60% F1-score on the Weibo-21 dataset.

LLM-based Virtual Student Response Generation and Performance Prediction

Independent Project
Educational AI Large Language Models Synthetic Data Knowledge Tracing DKT pyKT
  • Explored how large language models can generate virtual student response records while reducing dependence on sensitive real-world educational data.
  • Designed a lightweight pipeline inspired by Agent4Edu, including student profile construction and response action simulation.
  • Generated response records by allowing LLMs to simulate students with different ability levels on real mathematical exercises.
  • Aligned synthetic records with the ASSISTments format and trained deep knowledge tracing models under the pyKT framework.
