About Me

Data Science & Big Data Technology · AI for Education · Trustworthy AI

I am an undergraduate student at Chongqing University, majoring in Data Science and Big Data Technology. My academic interests center on AI for education, especially how data-driven and large-model methods can be used to understand learning behavior, generate educational data, and support reliable learning analytics.

My previous research has focused on trustworthy AI, including multi-agent reasoning, misinformation detection, multimodal learning, and knowledge-enhanced reasoning. These experiences shaped my current research direction: building educational AI systems that are not only effective, but also interpretable, robust, and useful in real teaching and learning scenarios.

Looking ahead, I hope to continue exploring learning analytics, knowledge tracing, and large language models for education, with the goal of developing intelligent systems that better model learners, support educational decision-making, and make personalized learning more trustworthy.

Research

Research projects are organized as academic-style entries, emphasizing research questions, personal contribution, methods, and outcomes.

Multi-Agent Fake News Detection System with Reflection and Iterative Learning
Project Leader · Advisor: Prof. Min Gao

Highlight: A multi-agent detection workflow with reflection and memory for more reliable misinformation detection.

Proposed a CrewAI-based detection framework that combines fast judgment, dynamic fact-checking, comprehensive reasoning, reflection triggers, and structured experience memory. The system improved F1-scores on PolitiFact and GossipCop compared with several baseline methods.

Multi-agent systems Reflection Experience memory Fake news detection
Multimodal Fake News Detection Based on MoE and Knowledge-enhanced Reasoning
Core Member · Advisor: Prof. Min Gao

Highlight: A multimodal expert-collaboration system for text, image, audio, and video misinformation signals.

Participated in designing a multimodal detection method with expert routing, decision-making, retrieval-augmented reasoning, and text-image-audio-video expert models. The model achieved 88.11% accuracy and 84.60% F1-score on the Weibo-21 dataset.

LLM-based Virtual Student Response Generation and Performance Prediction
Independent Project

Highlight: Generating virtual student response records to support knowledge tracing under limited real data.

Explored LLM-based generation of virtual student response records for educational modeling scenarios with limited or sensitive real data. The pipeline includes student profile construction, response behavior simulation, ASSISTments-format alignment, and pyKT-based knowledge tracing experiments.
